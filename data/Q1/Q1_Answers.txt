ANNAPURNA STORES - DATA MINING LAB 1
Q1 SUBMISSION

(a) PLATFORM AND LANDING

The platform uses MinIO as the object store, PostgreSQL as the relational database, and DuckDB as the analytical query engine.

Sales files are organized using store, year and month partitions:

sales/store=<store_id>/year=<year>/month=<month>/

This allows partition pruning so that a query for one store and one month does not need to inspect unrelated stores and months.


(b) IDEMPOTENCY

The loader is designed to handle re-sent files without creating duplicate business lines.

The safe deduplication unit is:

(bill_no, line_no)

Repeated identical records are removed before analytical processing.

The loader was executed repeatedly and row count and checksum were compared to verify that the result remains stable.


(c) DASHBOARD AND STAR SCHEMA

The fact table is maintained at sales-line grain.

The star schema contains:

fact_sales
dim_store
dim_product
dim_category
dim_date

Store name and address are maintained in the store dimension instead of being repeated for every sales line.

Revenue includes SALE, RETURN, DISCOUNT and VOID transactions.

TAX and TENDER are not revenue.

VOID records are retained because they cancel the corresponding original transaction.


(d) HISTORICAL PRICING

Product codes are not unique across all dates because product codes were reissued.

Therefore product matching uses the product code together with its validity period and product surrogate key.

Historical prices are obtained from price revision records according to the reporting date.

The same query structure can be used for different reporting periods by changing the reporting date.


(e) CROSS-SYSTEM QUERY

Sales data is stored in MinIO and master data is stored in PostgreSQL.

DuckDB can query the object-store sales data and PostgreSQL master data in the same analytical query without copying the source systems into each other.

EXPLAIN output provides evidence of the scan operations performed by DuckDB.


(f) RECONCILIATION

Analytical monthly revenue is compared with the finance monthly figures.

Differences are classified as:

1. Source-data problem
2. Revenue-definition difference
3. Pipeline bug

TAX and TENDER are excluded from revenue, while SALE, RETURN, DISCOUNT and VOID affect revenue according to their transaction meaning.

The reconciliation results are maintained as supporting evidence.
